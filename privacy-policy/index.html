6 Data Storage and Security

We take the security of your information seriously. Here is what we do — and what you should know.

  • Tool inputs are client-side only. Data you enter into our tools is processed in your browser and is not transmitted to any server we operate.
  • HTTPS everywhere. All pages on Multi-Tools are served over HTTPS, which encrypts data in transit between your browser and the server.
  • No user databases. We do not operate a user account system, so we do not store login credentials, passwords, or account profiles.
  • Minimal data at rest. The only data stored on servers we influence is what our analytics provider retains — aggregated, anonymized usage statistics.
⚠️
No Method Is Foolproof No data transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we apply industry-standard protections, we cannot guarantee absolute security. Use our tools with this understanding in mind.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Multi-Tools collect personal data?

Multi-Tools does not require you to create an account or submit personal information to use any tool. Tools such as the QR code generator process your input entirely in the browser and do not transmit data to our servers.

Does Multi-Tools use cookies?

We may use essential cookies required for site functionality and analytics cookies to understand how visitors use the site. We do not use advertising or marketing cookies. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Does Multi-Tools share my data with third parties?

We do not sell your personal data. We may share aggregated, non-identifiable usage statistics with analytics providers. We never sell, rent, or trade individual user data to third parties.

Is the data I enter into your tools stored anywhere?

No. Your tool input — for example, the URL you enter into the QR code generator — is processed locally in your browser using JavaScript. It is not sent to our servers and is not stored anywhere after you close or refresh the page.

Can I request deletion of my data?

Yes. Under GDPR and similar regulations, you have the right to request deletion of any personal data we hold. Contact us using the details in Section 11. Since we collect minimal data, most deletion requests can be fulfilled quickly. Note that analytics data is typically anonymized and cannot be linked back to an individual.

Do you track me across other websites?

No. We do not use cross-site tracking pixels, retargeting scripts, or advertising networks. Our analytics are limited to understanding on-site behavior (which pages users visit, how long they stay) using aggregated, anonymized data.

Is Multi-Tools safe for children to use?

Our tools do not require personal information and process all data locally in the browser. However, Multi-Tools is not specifically designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from this age group. Parents should supervise children's internet use as they see fit.
