Introduction

Multi Tools ("we", "our", "us") provides free online calculators, converters, generators, and productivity utilities at this website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Multi Tools, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Most Multi Tools utilities run entirely in your browser, meaning the text, numbers, or files you enter are processed on your own device and are never transmitted to our servers. Where a tool does need to send data (for example, a server-side conversion), we only process what is required to deliver the result and do not store it longer than necessary.

We may automatically collect limited technical information, such as your browser type, device type, approximate location (city or country level), and pages visited, through standard analytics logging. This information does not directly identify you by name.

How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the tools and pages on this site.
  • To understand which tools are most useful, so we can prioritize new features.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, abuse, or security incidents.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies & Local Storage

Multi Tools may use cookies or your browser's local storage to remember basic preferences, such as a recently used tool or display setting. These are not used to build an advertising profile of you. You can clear or block cookies at any time through your browser settings; doing so will not prevent you from using the tools, though some preferences may not be remembered.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services, such as website analytics or hosting infrastructure, to help operate this site. These providers may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies. We select providers that maintain reasonable data-protection standards, but we encourage you to review their policies directly for full details.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect any information that passes through our systems. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Because most tools process data locally in your browser, your files and text typically never leave your device.

Children's Privacy

Multi Tools is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when this policy was last revised. Continued use of Multi Tools after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact us at support@example.com.
