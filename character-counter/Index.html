What is a Character Counter?

A Character Counter is a free, browser-based tool that instantly measures the length and composition of any text. Simply type or paste your content and the tool immediately displays the total character count alongside a rich set of text statistics — no account required, no data ever leaves your browser.

Whether you are drafting a tweet, crafting an SEO meta description, writing an Instagram bio, or polishing a blog post, knowing your exact character and word counts is essential to maintaining platform compliance and readability.

Why Use a Character Counter?

  • 🐦
    Social Media Compliance

    Twitter/X enforces a strict 280-character limit, Instagram bios cap at 150 characters, and LinkedIn headlines allow up to 220. Exceeding these limits means your post gets cut off or rejected.

  • 🔍
    SEO Optimisation

    Search engines display meta titles up to around 60 characters and meta descriptions up to 160 characters. Staying within these limits ensures Google shows your full snippet, improving click-through rates.

  • ⏱️
    Readability Insights

    Estimated reading and speaking times help you gauge whether your content is the right length for your audience — crucial for emails, video scripts, and presentations.

  • 💾
    Auto-Save

    Your text is automatically saved in your browser's Local Storage, so you never lose work if you accidentally close the tab or refresh the page.

Pro Tips for Character Counting

01

Twitter / X Posts

Keep tweets under 280 characters. URLs automatically count as 23 characters regardless of their actual length. Use our live progress bar to stay in the green zone.

02

Meta Descriptions

Aim for 120–160 characters for the best Google snippet display. Shorter descriptions may leave valuable keyword real estate unused; longer ones get truncated mid-sentence.

03

SEO Meta Titles

Keep page titles between 50–60 characters. Lead with the most important keyword and brand name last. The progress bar turns yellow above 50 and red above 60 — your signal to trim.

04

SMS Messages

Standard SMS supports 160 characters. Messages beyond this split into multiple segments — each billed separately. Keep campaign texts tight to control costs and improve open rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Character Counter?

A Character Counter is a free online tool that counts the number of characters, words, sentences, paragraphs, and more in any text you type or paste. It helps writers, SEO professionals, and social media managers stay within platform limits and gauge content length at a glance.

How are words counted?

Words are counted by splitting the text on whitespace boundaries. Each contiguous sequence of non-whitespace characters is considered one word. Extra spaces and blank lines are ignored so the count accurately reflects meaningful content.

Does spaces count as characters?

Yes, spaces are included in the total character count because most social media platforms count spaces. We also provide a separate "Characters (without spaces)" metric so you can instantly see both values.

Can I check Twitter/X character limits?

Yes! The tool includes a dedicated Twitter/X limit checker with a progress bar up to 280 characters. The bar stays green while safe, turns yellow when you are approaching the limit, and turns red when you exceed 280 characters.

Can I check Meta Description length?

Absolutely. The tool includes a Meta Description progress bar set at 160 characters — the recommended limit for optimal display in Google search results. Keep it in the green zone for best SEO performance.

Is this tool free?

Yes, our Character Counter is completely free with no registration, no sign-up, and no usage limits. It runs entirely in your browser — no data is ever uploaded to a server, making it safe for sensitive content.
