What Is an SEO Audit Tool?

An SEO audit tool reads a webpage the way a search engine crawler does, then checks it against a known set of on-page best practices: title tag length, meta description, heading structure, image alt text, internal and external links, mobile viewport, and keyword usage. Instead of guessing why a page underperforms, an audit gives you a concrete, ordered list of what to fix.

Most people land here after typing some version of what is seo audit tool or what is a good seo site audit tool into Google. The short answer: it's a diagnostic step, not a ranking guarantee. Fixing what it flags removes friction — it doesn't replace good content, backlinks, or real user demand.

Why Run an SEO Audit

  • Catch technical gaps early — a missing title tag or a page set to "noindex" can silently keep a page out of search results.
  • Prioritize fixes — an audit ranks issues so you work on what actually moves the needle first.
  • Track keyword usage — a built-in keyword counter tool shows whether your focus term is missing, buried, or overused.
  • Sanity-check before publishing — running a free seo audit tool before a page goes live catches mistakes while they're still cheap to fix.

Why Each Check Matters

Title tag. This is usually the clickable headline in search results. Too short wastes an opportunity to describe the page; too long gets truncated with "…" and can hide your keyword entirely.

Meta description. Not a ranking factor on its own, but it's your pitch for the click. A blank or truncated description often gets replaced by a random snippet Google pulls from the page instead.

Heading structure. One H1 tells both readers and crawlers what the page is about. H2s underneath act like a table of contents, which is exactly what this audit tool checks for.

Image alt text. Alt text lets screen readers describe an image and gives search engines context they can't get from pixels alone — it's an accessibility fix and an SEO fix at the same time.

Canonical and robots tags. These two are easy to miss and expensive when missed: a wrong canonical can split ranking signals across duplicate URLs, and a leftover "noindex" from staging can quietly remove a live page from Google entirely.

Printable SEO Audit Checklist

Use this as a quick pre-publish pass before you even open the tool above:

  • Title tag is unique, 30–60 characters, and includes the focus keyword near the front
  • Meta description is 120–160 characters and reads like a reason to click
  • Exactly one H1, matching the page's actual topic
  • H2s break the page into scannable sections
  • Every meaningful image has descriptive alt text
  • Page has at least ~300 words of real, useful content
  • Canonical tag points to the correct URL
  • Viewport meta tag is present for mobile rendering
  • Robots meta is not set to "noindex" unless that's intentional
  • Focus keyword density sits roughly between 0.5% and 2.5%

Keyword Density, Fully Explained

A huge number of searches around this topic — google keyword density, keyword density google, keyword density for google — are people looking for an official number Google has never published. There isn't one. What exists instead is a practical range that experienced writers use as a gut check.

The Keyword Density Formula

The keyword density formula is simple:

(Keyword count ÷ Total word count) × 100 = Keyword density %

This keyword density calculation works for a single word or a full phrase — the tool above runs it automatically as part of the audit.

Best Keyword Density Range

DensityWhat it usually means
0%Keyword not found — the page may not clearly target the term
0.1%–0.5%Low — the term is present but thin
0.5%–2.5%Generally considered the best keyword density range
2.5%–3.5%High — worth a manual read-through
3.5%+Maximum keyword density exceeded — stuffing risk

Why "Best Keyword Density Tool" Results Vary

Searches for best keyword density and best keyword density tool often turn up different numbers from different sites, because each tool counts words slightly differently — some strip stopwords, some count phrases, some don't. That's normal. Use density as a directional signal, not an exact science.

A Worked Keyword Density Calculation Example

Say a page has 800 total words, and the phrase "seo audit tool" appears 6 times. The keyword density calculation is: (6 ÷ 800) × 100 = 0.75%. That sits comfortably inside the best keyword density range. If that same phrase appeared 30 times on the same 800-word page, density jumps to 3.75% — past the maximum keyword density line, and a strong sign to rewrite some of those repeats into natural variations.

Keyword Density vs. Word Density

Keyword density measures one specific term. Word density is broader — it's a full breakdown of how often every word or phrase on the page repeats. Running both together is useful: word density surfaces accidental over-repetition you didn't even set out to target, while keyword density confirms whether your actual focus term is under- or over-used.

Common On-Page Mistakes

Do

  • Write one clear, unique title tag per page
  • Use your focus keyword naturally in the first paragraph
  • Add descriptive alt text to every meaningful image
  • Keep one H1 per page

Avoid

  • Repeating the same phrase past the maximum keyword density point
  • Leaving the meta description blank
  • Publishing thin pages under ~300 words
  • Forgetting to remove a "noindex" tag before launch

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an SEO audit tool?

It's a checker that reads your page's HTML and reports on-page SEO issues — title tag, meta description, headings, alt text, and keyword density — so you know exactly what to fix.

What is the best free SEO audit tool?

The best free seo audit tool for a fast check is one that needs no signup and doesn't store your data. The tool at the top of this page works entirely in your browser.

What is the keyword density formula?

(Keyword count ÷ total word count) × 100. It's the same keyword density calculation used across most keyword density tools.

Is there a maximum keyword density I should stay under?

There's no official cutoff, but density above roughly 3%–3.5% for one phrase is a common warning sign of keyword stuffing.

Which SEO audit tool is best for beginners?

Beginners tend to do better with a free tool that explains results in plain language rather than raw scores — that's the approach used above.

What is a good SEO site audit tool for a small website?

For a small site, a lightweight, browser-based audit tool is usually enough — you don't need an enterprise platform to catch missing title tags, thin content, or a runaway keyword density.

How is keyword density different from keyword stuffing?

Keyword density is just a measurement. Keyword stuffing is what happens when that measurement gets pushed too high — when a phrase repeats so often it stops reading naturally and starts looking manipulative to both readers and search engines.
