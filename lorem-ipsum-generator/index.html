What Is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is placeholder text derived from a scrambled passage of Latin, used to fill a design mockup or document with realistic-looking copy before final content exists. It begins, almost universally, with the words "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit…" — a phrase so closely tied to the practice that the whole convention now carries its name.

Because it reads as language without reading as meaning, Lorem Ipsum lets everyone looking at a layout — designers, clients, developers — judge spacing, type size, and visual rhythm without getting distracted by the actual sentence they're looking at.

The History of Lorem Ipsum

The text traces back to a genuine work of classical Latin: Cicero's De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum, written in 45 BC. Sections of that text were scrambled, shortened, and altered by an unknown typesetter, most likely in the 1500s, to produce a specimen sheet for a printer's type samples — which is exactly why the passage looks like real prose at a glance but doesn't actually translate into coherent Latin sentences.

The text resurfaced in the 1960s when Letraset used passages of Lorem Ipsum on dry-transfer sheets for graphic designers, and it later became a built-in placeholder in early desktop publishing software — cementing it as the default filler text still used across the design and development world today.

Why Designers Use Lorem Ipsum

  • Neutral content: it doesn't carry a message, so nobody reviewing a layout gets distracted evaluating the copy instead of the design.
  • Natural letter distribution: its mix of short and long words approximates normal English or Latin-derived text far better than repeated filler like "text text text."
  • Universally recognized: anyone in publishing, design, or development instantly understands that Lorem Ipsum means "placeholder, not final."
  • Flexible length: it can be generated in any quantity, from a single word to dozens of paragraphs, to match any layout.

Best Practices & Common Mistakes

  • Do replace all placeholder text before a project ships — it should never reach production or print.
  • Do match the generated length to the real content you expect, so layout testing stays realistic.
  • Don't use Lorem Ipsum in accessibility testing that requires meaningful text, like screen-reader content reviews.
  • Don't forget that clients unfamiliar with the convention may need a quick explanation of what it is.
  • Do use the HTML output modes when you need markup-ready placeholder content for a live template.

Checklist

  • ☑ Output type matches what you're testing (paragraph, sentence, word, or character count)
  • ☑ Format matches your destination (plain text vs. HTML)
  • ☑ Length is realistic for the layout being tested
  • ☑ All placeholder text is removed before final delivery
