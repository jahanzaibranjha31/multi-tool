Keyword density, explained

Search consoles are full of people typing google keyword density, keyword density google, and keyword density for google — but Google has never published an official target number. What this seo audit tool (and most SEO practitioners) actually recommend is using density as a sanity check, not a rule to hit exactly.

What is the keyword density formula?

The standard keyword density formula is: (number of times the keyword appears ÷ total word count) × 100. Run it for your primary term and for close variants, since a real keyword counter tool should track more than one phrase at once.

What counts as the best keyword density?

There's no single best keyword density that works for every page. As a starting range, most seo audit tool checklists suggest keeping any one phrase under roughly 2.5% of total words, then adjusting based on how natural the copy reads out loud.

Is there a maximum keyword density I should stay under?

Once a single phrase repeats far more often than a person would naturally write it, search engines can read that as keyword stuffing. Treat 3%+ as a warning line, and use a word density breakdown across your whole page — not just one term — to catch it early.
